# 🗺️ Alex's Developer Adventure

> An interactive developer portfolio built as an explorable game world.

Welcome to my interactive portfolio.

Rather than presenting my experience through a traditional website, I wanted to create something that feels more like an adventure. Explore the world, interact with characters, discover projects, and learn more about my experience as a developer.

This project combines my interests in **software development, games, and interactive experiences** to create a portfolio that is as much a project to explore as it is a showcase of my work.

---

## ⚔️ The Concept

The idea behind the portfolio is simple:

**What if a CV was a world you could explore?**

Instead of navigating through a collection of pages, visitors can move around the environment and discover information through exploration and interaction.

Different areas of the world represent different parts of my professional experience, with NPCs, objects, and locations providing information along the way.

The aim is to make discovering my experience feel more engaging and memorable than scrolling through a traditional portfolio.

---

## 🌎 Explore the World

The portfolio is built around an explorable 2D world.

Visitors can:

- 🧭 Explore different areas
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Interact with NPCs
- 💬 Discover dialogue and information
- 📜 Learn about my experience
- 💼 Explore my projects
- 🛠️ Discover my technical skills
- 🔍 Find hidden details and Easter eggs

The world is designed to gradually reveal information rather than presenting everything at once.

---

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 NPCs & Dialogue

NPCs act as guides throughout the world.

Interacting with different characters reveals information about different parts of my career, projects, and experience.

Dialogue is presented through an RPG-style dialogue system, including:

- Character-specific dialogue
- Typewriter text effects
- Dialogue audio
- Interactive text boxes
- Dialogue progression
- Player interaction detection

The intention is to make conversations feel more like an actual game interaction rather than a standard website popup.
